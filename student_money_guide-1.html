Why Budgeting Matters

Budgeting helps students understand where their money goes. It makes it easier to control unnecessary spending, plan for important expenses, and build a regular saving habit.

Smart Saving Tips

📝 Track Every Expense

Write down your daily spending so you know where your money goes.

📅 Set a Weekly Limit

Decide how much you can comfortably spend each week.

🛍️ Avoid Unnecessary Purchases

Pause before buying something and ask whether you really need it.

💰 Save Before Spending

Keep a small amount aside as soon as you receive your money.
